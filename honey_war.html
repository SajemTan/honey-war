Information

For rules and other general information, please see this page.

The board contains coordinate indicators on each hex, in the form of a row of small dots on either side of each hex. The left is x, the right is y. Dots on the upper side of the hex are positive, and the lower side is negative. No dots represents 0.

The right column of the move and history lists contains move notation of the following form. Coordinates are expressed as pairs of letters mapped as [-5..5]=>[vwxyz-abcde] (that is, a literal hyphen is used for zero). a coordinate is prefixed with its semantic role: no prefix represents a non-attack destination, '>' represents an attack destination, '*' represents a non-moving attack target (including cavalry attack and retreat), '#' represents garrisoning, and '!' represents winning (destroying a castle). '>' and '*' are also followed by the symbol of the attacked piece.
